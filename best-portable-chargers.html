How we choose

We research specs, compare prices, and read long-term owner feedback and expert reviews. Here's what matters most for this category:

  • Real capacity (mAh / Wh) vs. size and weight
  • Charging speed — USB-C Power Delivery wattage
  • Number of ports and pass-through charging
  • Airline-safe capacity (under 100Wh)
  • Build quality and safety certifications

Frequently asked questions

What size power bank do I need?

10,000mAh charges most phones about twice. For laptops, look for 20,000mAh+ with 45W–100W USB-C PD.

Can I take a power bank on a plane?

In the US, power banks under 100Wh (about 27,000mAh) are allowed in carry-on bags — never in checked luggage.

Is fast charging bad for my battery?

No. Modern phones manage charging speed themselves to protect the battery.
