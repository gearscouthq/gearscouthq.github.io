Frequently asked questions

Do budget smartwatches work with iPhone?

Many do, but some features are limited on iPhone. Check compatibility before buying.

Are fitness trackers accurate?

For steps and heart rate they're generally good; treat calorie numbers as estimates.

Smartwatch or fitness band?

Bands are lighter with longer battery; smartwatches have bigger screens and more apps.
