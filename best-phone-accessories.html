Frequently asked questions

What is Qi2?

Qi2 is the newer wireless charging standard with magnetic alignment, similar to Apple's MagSafe.

Do I need a screen protector?

Modern glass is tough, but a cheap tempered-glass protector is still good insurance.

Which charger is best for my phone?

A USB-C PD charger of 20W–45W is a great choice for most modern phones.
