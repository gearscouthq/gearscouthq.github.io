How we choose

We research specs, compare prices, and read independent expert testing and long-term owner feedback. Here's what matters most for air fryers:

  • Capacity — basket size vs. how many people you cook for
  • Power (wattage) and maximum temperature for fast, crispy results
  • Coating — ceramic / PFAS-free vs. traditional non-stick
  • Ease of cleaning — dishwasher-safe parts
  • Counter space and noise

Frequently asked questions

Are air fryers healthier than deep frying?

Air fryers use hot circulating air and little or no oil, so food usually has much less fat than deep-fried food.

What size air fryer do I need?

4–5 quarts suits 1–2 people. A 6-quart basket works for most families of 3–4. Bigger households should look at dual-basket or 8+ quart models.

What does PFAS-free mean?

PFAS are 'forever chemicals' used in some traditional non-stick coatings. PFAS-free air fryers use alternatives such as ceramic coatings.

Basket or toaster-oven air fryer?

Basket air fryers are faster and crispier for fries and wings. Toaster-oven styles hold more food flat and can also toast and bake.
